03 — Logo Usage

Logo Rules

The Hansa Neuron logo combines a neural-brain icon with a clean wordmark. Always maintain clear space and use approved variants only.

Minimum clear space = X (cap-height of the "H") on all sides

✓ DOOn light/white backgroundsFull colour — preferred usage
✓ DOOn dark backgroundsIcon teal-300, wordmark white
✓ DOOn teal backgroundsAll-white reverse variant
✕
✗ DON'TColoured busy backgroundsNever place on Terracotta or patterned surfaces
Additional don'ts:

Do not stretch, rotate, recolour, add drop shadows, rearrange the icon and wordmark, or use below 120px wide (digital) / 30mm wide (print).

05 — Voice & Tone

How We Sound

Hansa Neuron communicates with the confidence of a specialist and the clarity of a trusted colleague.

🎯

Clear & Precise

We speak in plain language, even about complex AI. No jargon without explanation. Clinicians and non-clinicians should both understand us.

"Our AI flags anomalies 40% earlier than manual review."
"Our proprietary heuristic engine leverages multi-modal inference pipelines."
🤝

Trusted Partner

We position ourselves alongside clinicians, never above them. We augment human judgment — we don't replace it.

"Built to support your clinical decisions, not override them."
"Our AI outperforms doctors in 9 out of 10 cases."
🌱

Quietly Confident

We let evidence do the talking. We don't oversell. Our tone is assured but never arrogant — like a specialist who doesn't need to shout.

"Trusted by 50+ European clinics. Here's what they say."
"We are the #1 disrupting force in AI healthcare!"
🛡️

Responsible & Safe

We proactively address privacy and regulation. GDPR, CE marking, and data security aren't footnotes — they're part of the story.

"CE-marked, GDPR-compliant, and fully auditable by your clinical governance team."
"Don't worry about compliance — we handle all that."
