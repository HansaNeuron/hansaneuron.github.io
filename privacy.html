Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

The protection of your personal data matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Controller

The controller responsible for data processing within the meaning of the GDPR is:

Hansa Neuron
Hamburg, Germany
Email: info@hansaneuron.com

2. Data We Collect

We only collect personal data that you provide to us voluntarily, for example via our contact form.

When you submit the contact form, the following data is collected:

  • First name and last name
  • Email address
  • Company name (optional)
  • Your message

Processing is based on Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent) and Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures).

3. Purpose of Processing

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Process your enquiry and respond to you
  • Get in touch with you if you request a service

We do not share your data with third parties unless required to process your request or required by law.

4. Third-Party Service — Form Processing

Our contact form uses Web3Forms (web3forms.com) to securely transmit your message to our inbox. Web3Forms processes your submitted data solely to forward it to our email address and does not store it permanently.

For more information, see the Web3Forms Privacy Policy.

5. Retention Period

Your data is retained only as long as necessary to handle your enquiry, or as required by applicable legal retention obligations.

6. Hosting

This website is delivered via Cloudflare Pages (Cloudflare, Inc., San Francisco, USA). Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Server log data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) may be processed in the course of delivery. For details, see the Cloudflare privacy policy.

7. External Fonts

This website loads fonts via Bunny Fonts (BunnyWay d.o.o., Ljubljana, Slovenia — EU). Bunny Fonts is a privacy-friendly font service: no cookies are set, no IP addresses are logged and no data is transmitted to Google (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — interest in a consistent presentation).

7a. Website Check (quick scan)

On the "Website Check" page you can enter the address of a website, which is then automatically checked on a technical level (SSL, security headers, response time and technical compliance indicators, among others). As part of the check, our server function retrieves the homepage of the entered website as well as the imprint ("Impressum") and privacy policy pages linked from it, and analyses their publicly accessible content; only pages on the same domain are retrieved. The address you enter is transmitted to our server function (Cloudflare Pages Functions) solely to perform the check; neither the address nor the retrieved page content is stored permanently or used for advertising purposes. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performing the check you requested). Please only check websites you are authorised to test.

8. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Erasure of your data (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • Withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3) GDPR)

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at: info@hansaneuron.com

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. The competent authority for Hamburg is the Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

9. No Cookies or Tracking

This website does not use tracking cookies or analytics tools such as Google Analytics. No user profiles are created.

10. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy as needed to reflect changes in legal requirements or our services. The version published on this page is always the current one.
